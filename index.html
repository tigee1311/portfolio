  • Then video games, guessing where in the world a random street photo was taken, and a blog to write out what I actually thought.

Show up every day and the hard part gets easier. Happiest when I'm learning something, building something, or helping someone else do either.

WorldBench

Github

Robotics teams retrain their models all the time and sometimes a new version quietly gets worse. WorldBench runs the old and new checkpoint on the same test episodes, shows exactly which ones slipped, and gives a clear pass or fail. Open source, pip installable, built on LeRobot. Started at Founders, Inc.

Quantum key distribution

At Berkeley Lab's Quantum Systems Accelerator I worked with a small team on BB84, the protocol that lets two people share a secret key using single photons. We modeled how noise eats into the key, wrote Python simulations, drew the photonics setups in AutoCAD, and presented the results to researchers. Earlier I wrote Qiskit algorithms aimed at early signs of brain tumors in imaging data.

ColliderGuard

ColliderGuard

Github

Looks at 50,000 real particle collision events from CERN and flags the weird ones, but only when three different models agree. Each flag comes with a plain explanation of what made it strange. Python, scikit-learn, Streamlit.
