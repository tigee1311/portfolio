02Side quest

      
I built an antenna in my backyard and heard the ISS.

      
The International Space Station passes over Fremont a few times a day at 17,500 mph. It talks on 145.800 MHz. I cut and tuned a homemade antenna, pointed it at a pass window, and listened. Then the static cleared and there were voices from orbit.

      
No kit. No commercial dish. Copper, math, and a pass prediction.

WorldBench Founder, 2026

      
Catch a bad world-model checkpoint before it ships to a robot.

      
Robotics teams retrain world models constantly and ship regressions without noticing. WorldBench compares checkpoints across repeatable tasks and eval metrics, then gates deployment CI-style with pass/fail. Built on LeRobot with datasets like Yaskawa Untangle and Fractal.
